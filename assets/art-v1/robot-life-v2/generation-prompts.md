## 日常照顾

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: NEW transparent four-frame game sprite sheet, daily plant CARE, not harvest, for an existing 480x480 ESP32 train-garden game.
Input image is a character identity and hand-painted style reference only. Keep exactly the orange/ivory chipped squat robot, glass dome with sprout and ivy, orange round side module, dark cyan-eyed face, dark abdomen, three short mechanical lower limbs and an articulated gripper arm. Same proportions, same sprout, no redesign.
Produce precisely four equal cells in a TWO BY TWO grid, chronological top-left/top-right/bottom-left/bottom-right. SAME rigid shell size, SAME camera, SAME shell center in all frames. Transparent margins around each figure. No vertical bob baked into frames.
Action is routine caring for a living plant just outside frame at the right. Frame1 hover calmly with attentive open eyes, empty near-side gripper opens low and forward. Frame2 gently brace/support an unseen stem using an OPEN cupped gripper, body leans only 3 degrees. Frame3 softly brush or smooth the unseen leaf with the BACK of gripper, tiny gentle forearm motion, no pinching or picking. Frame4 open gripper returns close to frame1, content relaxed cyan eyes. All frames must keep the gripper visibly EMPTY. Small calm repetitive maintenance motion, not the look-down scan of inspection. Make it readable at small size. No tool, watering can, shovel, harvested leaf/fruit, resource drop, glow, scanning HUD or particle. Do not draw any target plant, pot, branch, floor or shadow. Only the robot and its own dome sprout/ivy. Preserve the reference's thin dark outline and warm painterly shading. No words, labels, grid lines, borders, scenery or watermark. Genuine alpha transparency. Distinct new poses, not a copy of the input atlas.

Same rigid head scale and center in every frame, constant camera and lighting. Same tucked hover silhouette, no legs walking, no zoom.
Harvest must clearly differ from routine empty-handed care and observation. Robot faces front three-quarter RIGHT and performs one complete harvest: frame1 extends open empty mechanical gripper right and slightly down toward unseen mature plant; frame2 closes gripper gently and lifts one small green harvested leaf/fiber sprig from the unseen plant; frame3 brings that small harvested sprig toward its body with a happy crescent cyan eye expression; frame4 gripper relaxes EMPTY near body after passing the sprig to inventory, content cyan eyes, ready to return to hovering. No target plant or pot in the sheet. The harvest piece is a tiny generic leaf/fiber sprig, not fruit or a basket. The player resource icon and flight-to-inventory effect will be rendered separately in code. No water, watering can, shovel, wrench, basket, resource UI or particle effects. No background, tree, floor, shadow, text, labels, grids, borders or watermark. Genuine alpha transparency around each single robot and generous margin. Keep shell dimensions identical all4frames; subtle limb motion only. Preserve the small hand-painted game sprite aesthetic.

Four frames in TWO BY TWO equal grid, chronological top-left/top-right/bottom-left/bottom-right. Same shell size, center, character model, camera and lighting in every cell.
