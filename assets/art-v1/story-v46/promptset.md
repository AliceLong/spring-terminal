# V4.6纪念花素材生成记录
工具：本会话内置image_gen；不是外部付费脚本。参考既有轨光花成熟素材，仅匹配绘画语言。原图位于masters/memorial-flower-atlas.png。

生成要求：ONE coherent four-frame growth sprite sheet, warm hand-painted post-apocalyptic botanical train game. Same small weathered cream ceramic flower pot with thin rusty orange rim, fixed side-three-quarter camera, same pot size, base and light direction across all four equal cells. Stages: tiny sprout; two developed leaves; closed bud; single five-petal ivory flower with warm yellow centre. Clear silhouettes readable at roughly48x64px. Transparent background and gutters; no labels, text, robot, furniture or scenery. Consistent pot geometry, no independent zoom per frame. Modest organic wear, muted moss greens, delicate warm highlights, no photorealism or hard black outlines.

导出：共同包围框、统一比例与底边；56×80，4张I8图片，共22,016字节。程序负责阶段切换，不增加逐帧大动画。新叶复用现有小叶素材；档案复用现有图标与文本卡片；看花/动物复用检查植物动作。无预录整部配音。
