## hover-flight-rest

Use case: stylized-concept. Production animation SPRITE SHEET, strict FOUR COLUMNS by THREE ROWS, EXACTLY12 isolated character sprites on true TRANSPARENT alpha. Each equal cell contains ONE whole robot, centered on SAME neck/body anchor, identical body scale and camera. No headings, cell lines, labels, UI, environment, floor, cast shadow, trails or speech text. Reference is CHARACTER IDENTITY ONLY: preserve orange and ivory weathered rounded box head, black oval face on right/front with two cyan eyes, orange circular side ear, clear glass terrarium dome with green sprout, three short mechanical limbs. Same warm hand-painted style. CONSISTENT three-quarter facing RIGHT in every frame; no turning into another model, no extra arms, no wheels, no propeller, no flames. Head and dome must stay same size in all12 frames. Sprites fill70% of their cell with transparent gutters. Limbs tuck underneath during HOVER, never spread into a walking gait. Four chronological poses per row, subtle controlled changes suitable for a looping animation. ROW1 HOVER IDLE: limbs tucked below body; neutral cyan eyes, slight blink frame3, tiny leaf movement, no drawn vertical translation. ROW2 TAKEOFF: frame1 landed three support limbs extended; frame2 limbs half retract; frame3 nearly tucked; frame4 fully tucked hovering. Keep body anchor fixed; height is controlled by game engine. ROW3 LANDED SLEEP: three limbs resting spread stably, sleepy closed curved cyan eyes; small head nod and sprout droop; do not draw Z text.
